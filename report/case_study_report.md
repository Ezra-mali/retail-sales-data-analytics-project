# Retail Sales Data Analysis Case Study


## Business Understanding

A significant amount of transactional sales data is produced by retail organizations. However, this data is frequently not utilized in decision-making processes in the absence of systematic analysis. The objective of this project is to evaluate retail sales data in order to enable data-driven business decisions, find revenue sources, and reveal performance trends.

### Stakeholders

- Sales Managers  
- Marketing Team  
- Operations & Inventory Team  
- Executive Management  

### Business Objectives

- Analyze historical retail sales performance  
- Identify patterns and trends across products and time  
- Determine factors influencing revenue changes  
- Provide actionable business recommendations  

### Success Criteria

- Clear definition and measurement of key performance indicators (KPIs)  
- Data-driven insights supported by evidence  
- Business-aligned recommendations  
- Effective visual communication of findings
