# Project Methodology

This project follows a structured data science methodology aligned with CRISP-DM principles.

## 1. Business Understanding
Define retail challenges, stakeholders, and success metrics.

## 2. Analytic Approach
Select descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive techniques aligned with business objectives.

## 3. Data Collection
Identify and obtain relevant retail transaction data.

## 4. Data Understanding
Explore dataset structure, quality, and distributions.

## 5. Data Preparation
Clean, transform, and engineer features.

## 6. Analysis & Modeling
Apply statistical analysis and forecasting techniques.

## 7. Evaluation
Assess insights against business objectives.

## 8. Communication & Deployment
Present findings via dashboards, reports, and cloud deployment.
